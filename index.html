A-Hub privacy policy

A-Hub is a private tool used by a small number of eBay sellers to manage their own listings and orders. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

With the seller's permission (given on eBay's own sign-in page), A-Hub reads and updates that seller's listings, business policies and orders through eBay's official APIs. Order data includes buyers' names and delivery addresses, which are used only to fulfil and track those orders.

How it is stored

Data is stored in the seller's own A-Hub installation. eBay access tokens are stored encrypted. Data is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for marketing.

Deletion

When eBay notifies us that a user's account was closed, that user's personal data is deleted. Sellers can disconnect A-Hub from their eBay account at any time, which revokes its access.

Contact

Questions: contact the seller who operates this A-Hub installation through eBay messages.
